## 2024-05-19 - Missing useMemo causes full array iteration on every render
**Learning:** Found that `components/home/ScentExplorer.tsx` was recalculating `activeMood` and `matchingProducts` (which involved `.find` and `.filter` over arrays) on every single render cycle, even when the relevant state `activeCategory` hadn't changed. This is particularly problematic in components that use contexts or custom hooks like `useDayNight` which can trigger frequent re-renders independent of the component's internal state.
**Action:** Wrap derived calculations (especially array iterations) in `useMemo` hooks, with explicit dependency arrays. Always look for `.filter`, `.map`, `.reduce`, and `.find` in the main body of functional components and consider memoizing them.
